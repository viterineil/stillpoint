# Code of conduct

Stillpoint's community should be a place for careful, candid, and respectful
technical collaboration.

Participants are expected to:

- critique ideas and evidence without attacking people;
- welcome questions and different levels of experience;
- disclose assumptions, limitations, and conflicts of interest;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and unwanted personal attention;
- protect private information encountered in logs, configurations, and reports;
- accept maintainer decisions on community safety and project scope.

Unacceptable conduct may be removed, and repeated or severe behavior may lead
to temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces. Report conduct issues
privately to the repository maintainers. Security vulnerabilities should follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
